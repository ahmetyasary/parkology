**Description**:
Welcome to Parkology, the cheerful parking puzzle where cute little blocks drive around the loop and find their way home!

Tap a lane to send its front block onto the loop. It drives around the parking lot and parks behind a block of its own color. Fill a column with one color and watch it pop. Clear the whole lot to win!

Sounds easy? The loop holds only 5 blocks at a time. Send the wrong ones and the traffic jams. Think a move ahead, keep the loop flowing and enjoy the satisfying rush when the last blocks race home.

• No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases

Park, pop and relax. Get Parkology now!
